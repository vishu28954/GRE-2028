# 1. Astute

## Word

**ASTUTE** *(adjective)*

## Pronunciation

```text
uh-STOOT
```

## English Meaning

Quick and clever at understanding a situation, especially in a practical or strategic way.

## Hindi Meaning

```text
चतुर / सूझ-बूझ वाला / परिस्थिति को जल्दी समझने वाला
```

## 🧠 Visual Association

![Astute — strategic chess thinking](https://es.chessbase.com/portals/all/2018/12/bestofpics/Fabiano%20Caruana-al.JPG)

*Visual source: ChessBase*

Imagine a chess player studying the board and immediately noticing a move that will create an advantage three turns later.

Everyone else sees pieces. He sees opportunity.

That is **ASTUTE**.

## Memory Hook

```text
Astute = sharp + strategic
```

## Synonyms

```text
shrewd
sharp
perceptive
clever
savvy
```

## Antonyms

```text
naive
obtuse
foolish
unperceptive
```

## Usage Paragraph

The investor made an **astute** decision to sell the company’s shares before the market declined. She had noticed several small warning signs that others dismissed. Her success came not from luck but from reading the situation quickly and intelligently.

## GRE Connections / Distinctions

```text
Astute → strategically clever
Sagacious → deeply wise
```

**Astute** often has a practical, strategic flavor.

## Hindi Meaning

# Important GRE Traps

## 1. Astute vs Perspicacious

```text
Astute → strategically clever
Perspicacious → unusually perceptive
```

An **astute negotiator** sees how to gain advantage.  
A **perspicacious analyst** notices hidden patterns.

---

## 2. Sagacious vs Judicious

```text
Sagacious → deeply wise person/advice
Judicious → sensible, balanced decision
```

---

## 3. Perspicacious vs Discerning

```text
Perspicacious → detects subtle/hidden things
Discerning → distinguishes quality/differences
```

---

## 4. Naive vs Gullible

```text
Naive → inexperienced
Gullible → easily deceived
```

A person can be naive without actually being fooled.

---

## 5. Gullible vs Credulous

```text
Gullible → easily tricked
Credulous → believes too readily
```

---

## 6. Ingenuous vs Ingenious

```text
Ingenuous → innocent / sincere
Ingenious → clever / inventive
```

One letter creates a completely different meaning.

---

## 7. Credulous vs Incredulous

```text
Credulous → too willing to believe
Incredulous → unwilling / unable to believe
```

Classic GRE trap.

---

## 8. Obtuse vs Naive

```text
Obtuse → slow to understand
Naive → inexperienced
```

---

## 9. Myopic vs Imprudent

```text
Myopic → narrow / short-term perspective
Imprudent → poor judgment about consequences
```

---

## 10. Rash vs Foolhardy

```text
Rash → too quick
Foolhardy → recklessly bold despite danger
```

| **Myopic** | short-sighted thinking | tunnel vision | myopic ↔ farsighted |
| **Imprudent** | unwise judgment | risky decision | imprudent ↔ prudent |
| **Rash** | acts too quickly | impulsive leap | rash ≈ impetuous |
| **Foolhardy** | recklessly bold | rooftop stunt | stronger danger element |
