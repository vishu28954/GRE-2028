For our GRE work, I’d use the model settings strategically rather than keeping the highest reasoning level on all the time.

For **most vocabulary learning, revision, synonym distinctions, Hindi explanations, usage paragraphs, and normal quizzes**, **Instant** is enough. On Plus, Instant is still powered by GPT-5.6 Sol, and it can automatically reason more when needed if Higher Intelligence is enabled. ([OpenAI Help Center][1])

For **GRE Sentence Equivalence, Text Completion logic, subtle synonym traps, difficult Reading Comprehension passages, error analysis, or when you want me to build a very polished vocabulary group**, switch to **Medium**. That is probably the best default for serious GRE study because it gives a better balance between quality and usage. Plus includes Medium and High reasoning with GPT-5.6 Sol. ([OpenAI Help Center][1])

Use **High** selectively for the hardest work: dense RC passages, ambiguous TC/SE questions, detailed study-plan redesigns, or when you want a meticulous analysis of why multiple answer choices are tempting. I would not use High for things like “quiz me on yesterday’s 15 words” because you gain little from the extra reasoning there.

A practical setup for us would be:

* **Instant:** daily vocabulary review, recall tests, definitions, simple explanations
* **Medium:** new 15-word GRE groups, SE/TC practice, distinction drills, most tutoring
* **High:** hard RC, difficult GRE logic, deep error analysis, mock-test postmortems

I’d make **Medium our main GRE tutoring mode** and drop to Instant for lighter revision. That should preserve your reasoning allowance without sacrificing much quality.

Also, manually selecting Medium or High uses the GPT-5.6 reasoning allowance, while automatic reasoning that happens while Instant is selected does **not** count against that manually selected reasoning allowance. ([OpenAI Help Center][1])

For your specific workflow, I’d use **Medium tomorrow for Group 3**, because we need careful semantic clustering, GRE distinctions, and polished teaching content. Then for the short revision quiz afterward, Instant would be perfectly adequate.
